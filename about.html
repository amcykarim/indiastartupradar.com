About

Useful business tools, clearly explained.

IndiaStartupRadar is becoming a free online tools platform for Indian startups, small businesses, freelancers, founders, marketers and ecommerce sellers.

Our approach

We aim to publish lightweight tools with transparent formulas, examples and practical context. Whenever possible, calculations run in the browser without accounts, paid APIs or uploaded financial data.

Quality standard

We will not publish thin placeholder tools. Each tool should solve a real problem and explain its assumptions and limitations.
