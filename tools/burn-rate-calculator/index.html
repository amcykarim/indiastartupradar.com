Formula and methodology

Gross burn = Operating expenses ÷ Months; Net burn = (Opening cash − Closing cash) ÷ Months

The calculator applies this stated method directly to the values you enter. Keep amounts and time periods consistent.

Worked example

₹12,00,000 expenses over 6 months is ₹2,00,000 monthly gross burn.

How to use
