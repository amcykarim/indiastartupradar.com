Formula and methodology

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)

This calculator uses the values you enter and does not add fees, taxes or external assumptions.

Worked example

₹10,00,000 at 12% for 36 months gives an EMI of about ₹33,214.

How to use
