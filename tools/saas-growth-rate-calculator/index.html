Formula and methodology

MRR growth = (Ending MRR − Starting MRR) ÷ Starting MRR × 100

This calculator applies the stated formula directly. Use consistent accounting periods and definitions.

Worked example

MRR growing from ₹5,00,000 to ₹6,00,000 is 20% growth.
