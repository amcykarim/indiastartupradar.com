Formula and methodology

Enterprise value = Annual revenue × Multiple; Equity value = Enterprise value + Cash − Debt

The calculator applies this stated method directly to the values you enter. Keep amounts and time periods consistent.

Worked example

₹1 crore revenue at 5×, plus ₹20 lakh cash and ₹10 lakh debt, estimates ₹5.1 crore equity value.

How to use
