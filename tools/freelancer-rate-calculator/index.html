Formula and methodology

Hourly rate = (Desired annual income + Annual expenses) ÷ Annual billable hours

This calculator uses the values you enter and does not add fees, taxes or external assumptions.

Worked example

₹12 lakh desired income plus ₹2 lakh expenses over 1,000 billable hours requires ₹1,400 per hour.
