Formula and methodology

Retention = (Ending customers − New customers) ÷ Beginning customers × 100

This calculator applies the stated formula directly. Use consistent accounting periods and definitions.

Worked example

500 beginning, 480 ending and 30 new customers gives 90% retention.

How to use

  1. Enter values from the same reporting period.
  2. Select Calculate.
  3. Review the result alongside the formula and limitations.
