Formula and methodology

Weighted pipeline = Σ(Opportunity value × Close probability)

This calculator uses the values you enter and does not add fees, taxes or external assumptions.

Worked example

Three ₹1 lakh opportunities at 20%, 50% and 80% have ₹1.5 lakh weighted value.
