Formula and methodology

Total growth = (End − Start) ÷ Start; compound growth = (End ÷ Start)^(1/periods) − 1

This calculator uses the values you enter and does not add fees, taxes or external assumptions.

Worked example

Growth from ₹10 lakh to ₹15 lakh over 3 periods is 50% total growth.
